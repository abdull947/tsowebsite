About Us

Our history, values, and the story of Thongmus Students Organization

Who We Are

Thongmus Students Organization (TSO) was founded in 2010 by passionate students from the Thongmus region of Gilgit-Baltistan. We have grown into a strong platform serving hundreds of students, believing in unity, education, and community service.

Our History

Established in 2010, TSO began as a small gathering of students united by a common vision. Over time it evolved into a formal organization with a structured cabinet, regular programs, and a growing membership base spanning universities across Pakistan.

Core Values

Education Unity Service Integrity Leadership Cultural Pride

Our Achievements

Since 2010, TSO has organized numerous scholarship programs, cultural events, academic workshops, and community welfare initiatives. We connect students from Thongmus studying across Pakistan and beyond.
