Our Vision

To build a self-reliant, educated, and united community of Thongmus students who contribute meaningfully to society and carry forward their cultural heritage with pride.

  • Empowering youth through quality education
  • Building bridges between regions and generations
  • Fostering leadership and civic responsibility
Our Mission

To provide a supportive platform where students from Thongmus can connect, collaborate, grow academically, and give back to their community through service and advocacy.

  • Organizing scholarship and welfare programs
  • Promoting cultural events and awareness
  • Creating networks for career and academic support
